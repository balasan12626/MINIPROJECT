## Round 3 (2026-10-10) — transfer to larger instances and under noise
- New experiment `exp_p1_size.py` (data `final/data/p1_size.json`): the angles fixed at 12 qubits were applied unchanged
  to 10 new 15-qubit instances (mean and CVaR objectives) and 10 new 18-qubit instances, against per-instance training;
  and fixed vs trained p=4 XY circuits were run through the Pauli-trajectory noise model (200 runs, eps 0.005/0.01).
- New subsection V-D "Transfer to Larger Instances and Under Noise" and Table V (`tab_size`), generated by
  `post_p1_size.py`. The sentence on significance is chosen by the data; all other claims are asserted.
- Results: the fixed angles never fell below 0.176 (15 q) / 0.056 (18 q), whereas per-instance training collapsed below
  0.01 on 3/10 and 6/10 instances. Mean P_opt was higher but **not significantly** (p = 0.105 and 0.557, Wilcoxon).
  Success within 32 shots: 63.2% → 99.9% (15 q) and 39.1% → 91.5% (18 q). At eps = 0.01: 0.182 fixed vs 0.108 trained
  (better on 8 of 10).
- The abstract, contribution 3 and the limitations were updated (the size test stops at 18 qubits, 10 instances per size).
- Build: 6 pages, 247-word abstract, no overfull boxes, ALL CHECKS PASSED, references in citation order.
